# 2. Evidence of relevance

## 2.1 Approach

Evidence was collected through discussions with domain experts, including the pilot client, and through observation of an existing process (the client's content brief). The target is 3 to 5 discussions with user representatives or a substantive agreement with the actual client. Current status: 2 expert discussions held; requirements were approved by the clients.

## 2.2 Expert discussions

| Expert | Role | Company | What the discussion confirmed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Altay | Founder | Altay Santeh Opt (pilot client, Astana) | Manager overloaded: many requests, slow replies |
| Nikolai Salo | Founder | Salo Marketing (US home-services marketing) | The same overload at US home-services clients |

## 2.3 Observed process and available data

The client's content brief (shooting checklist for nine Reels) shows how leads reach the business: each Reel ends with a call to write a keyword in Direct or WhatsApp (ПОДБОР, КОМПЛЕКТ, КАРТРИДЖ, ФИТИНГ, РАДИАТОР, ПОДКЛЮЧЕНИЕ) and to send a photo and sizes. Inbound chats are therefore expected to be short, free-text and to miss technical details, which the agent must ask for.

## 2.4 Findings and their status

| Finding | Status | Informs requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Reels ask for a keyword, photo and sizes in Direct | Verified (client content brief) | FR-04, FR-13 |
| One manager, many requests, not all answered in time | Reported by Altay | FR-07, FR-08, NFR-01 |
| The same overload at US home-services clients | Reported by Nikolai Salo | NFR-08 |
| A faster first reply raises conversion | Assumption | Measured against the human baseline (section 9) |

Status legend: **Verified** means confirmed by a document or data; **Reported** means stated in a discussion and not yet measured; **Assumption** means to be tested in the evaluation.

## 2.5 Evidence still to collect

- An additional discussion with a sales manager (a direct user of the lead cards).
- Anonymized real dialogues for the test set, with written consent (section 9).
